WHO WE ARE

More than code.
A culture of making.

BOSC brings people together to create, improve, document, test, and maintain open-source technology.

Whether you're writing your first line of code or maintaining a project, there is a place to learn, contribute, and grow. We use real projects to build practical skills and explore technology that responds to real needs.
